# Candidate semantic forensic

Owner qualitative reading of the authorized Optimize examples, golds, initial and candidate outputs, all 380 gradients, all 50 Patterns, seven exact local-parent diffs, representatives and retrieved Memory. No provider judge; zero model calls. These are post-hoc descriptive observations, with no access to held-out raw samples.

The stable finding is `NO_CLEAR_SPECIALIZATION_SEMANTICS`. F01/F02/F05/F06 are broad solving/verification procedures. F03/F04 contain conditional formulas spanning several subjects. F07 carries training-instance constants and answer content. Gains overlap heavily and cross subject boundaries; they do not demonstrate four coherent complementary specialist mechanisms.

These contexts were present at generation, but this observational evidence cannot assign a causal effect to Pattern, Memory, prompt length or any particular sentence. F05/F07 mutate the minimal prompt directly. Every deployed initial-team prompt is the same 18-character minimal prompt. Local fixed/broken columns refer to the actual local mutation parent; Full retained/lost columns refer to the initial deployed member correct set.

Losses recur across candidates: a coefficient-sign flip, an incomplete solution set, invalid or non-equivalent telescoping results, constrained-extremum errors, and numeral-base subtraction errors. Some losses are terminal-invalid outputs; others remain validly formatted wrong answers. This directly establishes collateral changes in final outputs, not the hidden reasoning that caused them.

F03 acquires an exact symbolic expression that the frozen evaluator accepts as equivalent; this is coverage under the existing evaluator, without evidence that the numerical subtraction was explicitly worked out. F07 directly includes training-instance values and answer information in its prompt, including content associated with a gained prime-count example. Its gain must not be presented as evidence of abstract transfer. Historical evidence and method admission remain unchanged.

Some accepted gradients and Patterns request comparison with an unavailable reference solution. Other hypothetical diagnoses assert a particular branch, independence or domain interpretation without a demonstrated derivation. Deterministic contract acceptance therefore does not establish semantic usefulness. The manual diagnostic omits reference-dependent instructions, instance content and unproved branch prescriptions.

Observed recurrent reusable behaviors motivating the independent manual design are: preserving algebraic structure and feasible branches; constructing exhaustive, properly weighted sample spaces; deriving a consistent geometric model and requested region; and respecting integer structure and numeral representation. This taxonomy was formed after reading the data, and is not evidence that A4 generated such a decomposition.

No generalization, global impossibility, or method-causal claim follows from these development-set observations.
