Supplier monetary reservations enter the durable Token journal and exact scope
identity. They reserve input/output bounds plus physical-request fees before
transport, charge conservative fallback on unknown usage/crash, and fail closed
before a new request exceeds either cap. Rates use integer currency millionths
and do not assume cache discounts. They require actual supplier terms; conservative
accounting is distinct from an invoice or undocumented upstream charges.

inventory, read-only hash-chained Token/fee journal and response receipts before
granting held-out access. A Solver/Validation-only broker rejects every search
role and Test. Search never receives held-out outcomes. An engineering-aborted
parent cannot be evaluated; a valid resource truncation can evaluate only its

as a selected smaller evaluation or used to justify a retry.
