# V2.7 pre-cluster continuation: execution report

The frozen run completed 50 optimization opportunities and five atomic Commits. Team Vote rose from **118/150 to 125/150** and Oracle Coverage from **124/150 to 131/150**. This is positive observed performance on the adaptive development support in this one run; it does not establish independent generalization or the causal contribution of Gradient, Pattern, Memory or any other component.

The five Commit opportunities were 2, 7, 11, 24 and 27. Their target members were 2, 5, 3, 5 and 5 (one-based). Final member correct counts were 111, 116, 122, 115 and 124, against initial counts 111, 115, 113, 115 and 112. Final output validity was 743/750 versus 738/750 initially. Three of five members changed prompts. Opportunity 24 increased Oracle without Vote improvement; opportunity 27 improved Vote without Oracle improvement. These are separate observed team metrics, not evidence that the latter change causally converted the former coverage.

## Execution and cost

The process terminated at the frozen **50-opportunity resource ceiling**. Its scientific stopper requires four complete eligible-member epochs without Commit. Three complete epochs had accrued; the final suffix contained 23 consecutive no-Commit opportunities. Scientific stopping was not reached. The runner completed and sealed its files, but the formal search is resource-truncated.

The continuation made 8,823 provider attempts and charged 20,023,235 Tokens (all provider-reported; zero conservative fallback and zero reserved at close). The sealed parent contributed 889 attempts and 1,055,236 charged Tokens (882,887 provider-reported and 172,349 conservative fallback). Combined: 9,712 attempts and 21,078,471 charged Tokens. Monetary tracking was disabled and the cumulative Token ceiling was null. The continuation used 8,377 Optimize and 446 Shadow attempts; Validation and Test calls were zero. Fifty-four exact parent optimizer responses were revalidated and imported without new calls.

## Integrity and limits

The independent terminal audit passed across 15,957 inventoried files. Request wires, response receipts and ledger entries matched; the startup and source identities matched; all 750 initial profiles remained on five distinct member lanes; the reconstructed initial team exactly matched the sealed parent. The parent evidence and historical scores were unchanged. No Validation or Test data were accessed.

This is one resource-truncated, single-arm adaptive development run. Its five commits and Optimize/Shadow improvements support descriptive within-run observations only. No independent generalization, statistical comparison to a concurrently controlled baseline, method-component ablation or causal component effect was measured. The next research priority is a separately preregistered, adequately resourced comparison with an independent post-search evaluation; this result alone should not be promoted as a supported method claim.
