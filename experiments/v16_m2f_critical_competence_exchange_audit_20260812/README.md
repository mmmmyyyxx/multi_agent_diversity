# M2F Critical-Competence Exchange Audit

This API-free audit reuses the seven frozen Parent / Source M20 / Repaired M2F
rollouts. It leaves the preregistered M2F classifier unchanged:
`PIVOTAL_COMPATIBILITY_FAILED` remains the formal result of that probe.

The audit adds the missing symmetric side of the pivotal-loss diagnostic. A
critical gain is a parent-wrong case where the candidate makes the target
correct and the resulting fixed-peer team gives that target an exclusive
`unique` or `pivotal` role. Unique is classified before pivotal, matching the
historical collateral audit's mutually exclusive role hierarchy. Critical net
is descriptive only and is not a new reward or acceptance rule.

Relative to the same parents:

- Source M20 produced 28 critical gains and 10 critical losses, net `+18`.
- Repaired M2F produced 35 critical gains and 13 critical losses, net `+22`.
- M2F's gains comprise 25 new pivotal and 10 new unique cases.
- M2F oracle coverage changed by `+10` with no oracle losses.
- M2F vote exchange was 25 gains and 13 losses, net `+12`.
- Of 15 M2F responsibility repairs, 7 became pivotal support and all 7 directly
  converted an incorrect parent vote to a correct vote. The other 8 restored
  target correctness without a critical role.

Therefore the increase from 10 to 13 old pivotal losses is accompanied by
larger creation of new useful complementary competence. On these seven frozen
candidates, M2F exhibits critical-competence reallocation rather than net
critical-competence loss. This does not retroactively change the frozen
classifier, authorize a training pilot, or establish population-level efficacy.

The audit used no API/model calls and read no validation or test data. Event
rows containing question hashes remain private; the published report contains
only pair-level hashes and aggregate counts.
