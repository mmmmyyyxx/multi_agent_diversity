# M20 Collateral Structure Audit

Read-only, zero-API audit of the 14 evaluated M20 candidates from the
protocol-gate-PASS Generic-vs-M20 fixed-parent probe.

The seven target-regression candidates are not isolated accidental failures:
four are broad degradation and three lose pivotal competence. All three
pivotal-overwrite candidates also meet the broad-degradation threshold.
Across regression candidates, 13 assigned-responsibility gains accompany 77
non-responsibility losses; 10 losses are pivotal and 67 are stable correct
competence. No unique or fragile loss was observed.

This supports a coarse global-update / competence-retention bottleneck. It does
not support adding preservation examples back into proposal context, changing
responsibility representation, or changing the frozen Study-B conclusion.

Published files contain only hashes, counts, roles, margins, and provenance.
They exclude prompts, questions, answers, raw responses, caches, credentials,
endpoints, validation/test data, and absolute paths.
