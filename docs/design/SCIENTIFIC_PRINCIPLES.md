# Scientific interpretation principles

These cross-benchmark principles constrain research claims. They add no runtime
threshold, optimizer objective, parser rule, scheduler or authorization. Current
implementation is resolved through [CURRENT_SPEC](CURRENT_SPEC.md); benchmark
scoring and experiment settings have narrower authorities.

## Useful complementarity

The research endpoint is team task performance under the benchmark's frozen
aggregation. Member differences matter when they provide actual correct coverage
or help that aggregation. Prompt distance, wording, response disagreement and
different error patterns alone do not establish useful complementarity.
Oracle coverage measures whether any member succeeds; aggregation measures the
team's actual selected output. Report both where the benchmark defines them.
An Oracle gain is coverage evidence, not automatically an aggregation gain.

## Measurement and preservation

Distinguish output validity, member task correctness, correct coverage and team
performance. Restoring presentation validity alone is not mathematical repair.
Preservation must name its support and metric: a frozen aggregate member score
floor is not per-example correctness monotonicity, preservation of general
ability outside that support, or a zero-format-regression requirement.
Whether target-only progress, collateral loss or validity regression can be
admitted is determined by the benchmark and bound transition contract.

## Search, admission and stopping

Local measured candidates are search evidence; local gains do not establish
team gains. Search continuation and deployment admission are distinct. The
current version determines which legal evaluated candidates advance locally;
its Full and Shadow contracts determine deployment. Do not insert an unofficial
local gate or infer efficacy from the number of commits.
Engineering completion, local repair, deployed team improvement, the formal
scientific stop and independent generalization are distinct findings. A resource
ceiling is operational truncation. Even a formal no-commit stop establishes only
the frozen policy's stopping criterion, not global optimality or generalization.

## Observable evidence

Gradient diagnosis and responsibility are repair hypotheses from observable
responses, reference evidence and measured team states. They do not reveal hidden
reasoning or establish causal attribution. Patterns must describe transferable
corrections; source-specific answers and solution fragments cannot enter them.
Passing a bounded leakage guard is not a proof of universal semantic safety.

Optimize affects search. Shadow is an adaptive admission capability, not
independent validation; only authorized feedback can cross its boundary.
Post-freeze Validation and sealed Test have separately frozen access policies.
Raw held-out data cannot enter optimization. Component efficacy needs an
appropriate intervention; a successful bundled run cannot attribute its gain
to an individual component. Validity is audited separately from efficacy.

Historical source, scores, reports and classifiers retain their original
meaning. New diagnostics add separate evidence and never rewrite frozen results.
