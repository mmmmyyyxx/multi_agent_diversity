# V2.8 Scoped Edit Experience and Layer1 Evidence Feedback

Method identity: `unified_team_prompt_search_v2_8_scoped_experience_v1`.
This amendment changes optimizer-visible experience and local generation inputs.
It establishes no real-model utilization, candidate-quality or efficacy result.
Frozen V2.7 experiments remain evidence under their original source; this method
requires fresh source, binding, cache and single-use authorization identities.

## Scope and invariants

Unified Team Prompt Search remains the sole active research architecture.
Layer2 owns responsibility, opportunity and target selection; Layer1 owns local
prompt search. Gradient remains an observable-error repair hypothesis and Pattern
an abstract transferable direction. Team Vote and useful complementarity remain
the objective; general diversity has no independent objective or admission role.

Responsibility/WHO, Gradient generation and abstraction/partition semantics,
Solver/model/provider/decoding/scoring, aggregation, Full/Shadow admission and
the formal stopper are unchanged. Role/Strategy/Answer remain editable. Sampling
remains disjoint Mutation3/SearchValidation3/Probe6; six Reflection draws, 42
local metric evaluations, four exports, two Full promotions and the 3000-character
rendered prompt bound remain. Every safe evaluated edit still advances the local
parent; neutral/negative local results do not silently gate outer candidates.
No Preservation Gradient, Preservation Panel, new capability-retention veto,
Pareto archive, scheduler replacement or extra API generation is introduced.

## Experience recording and evidence scope

Each current edit record has `SCOPED_EDIT_EXPERIENCE_RECORD_V1`, the original
opportunity team/parent identity, Mutation metadata types, actual reconstructable
edit chain, root/immediate-parent local comparisons and later Probe/Full outcomes.
Only measured Optimize evidence enters private mathematical experience. Shared
risk retains the existing closed generic signatures, with no raw strategy,
question, reference, response or sample-level Shadow evidence.

Actual edit operations and concepts are derived from changed spans, not all
inherited instructions or a model's unverified change summary. When available,
a complete surface sentence/clause intersecting the last child edit is shown as
`last_step_instruction`. It is a last-step description, not the entire chain or
an isolated explanation of Full results. Claims and measurements must not be
confused: the repair hypothesis is intent; coverage deltas are observations.

Full and local outcomes have named scopes and denominators. Compact Full cards
retain Full fixed/lost/invalid/target/Vote measurements and the small local-root
net observation; an unmeasured scope is absent. A Full-supported candidate rejected
by Shadow is explicitly labeled as rejected admission, not committed success.
Parent/child prompt and team matches refer to the ORIGINAL opportunity root, not
the later temporary local parent. Different parents/peers limit applicability.

## Retrieval, selection and delivery

Private retrieval stays member-isolated. Query cues are exact Pattern/hypothesis,
shared normalized terms, actual edit concepts, source task-type labels, an optional
edited-block hint and parent/child/team identity matches. These deterministic cues
are relevance heuristics, not semantic or causal proofs. Task-type labels can be
coarse; equality of topics does not establish an equivalent failure mechanism.

The most relevant measured Full record receives priority over recent local-only
records. A complementary supported/refuted Full record is considered next, then
recent local feedback. There is no unconditional preference for negative Full
experience. Exact duplicate observations may be omitted from a view; conflicting
outcomes are not collapsed. Retention/storage limits and the shared-risk mechanism
are unchanged: this amendment does not create an unbounded experience archive.

The canonical ASCII JSON context bound remains 1200 characters, private/shared
top-k limits remain three, private storage remains 24 per member with at most five
Full-refuted records, and shared storage remains 48. Minimal named measurements
and provenance are packed first. A complete last-step instruction for the primary
Full record is protected when it fits; additional cards and optional whole text
use remaining capacity. The instruction ceiling is 600 characters inside, not in
addition to, the 1200-character total. A whole text field that cannot fit is omitted,
never silently sliced into an incomplete mathematical rule. Shared generic risks
cannot evict selected Full evidence. Not every historical record or text can fit.

For a nonempty relevant Full set, failure to deliver its primary compact card is
an integrity error. The journal records bank/relevance counts, delivered IDs,
omissions, exact serialized length and complete-instruction delivery. Presence
in a bank, selection, wire delivery and model utilization are separate facts.

## Layer1 input and iteration feedback

Reflection receives the generalized selected Pattern and accepted diagnostic
fields for only its matching Mutation examples: observed failure, diagnosis,
reusable correction, advisory block and expected effect. These are labeled as
original-opportunity observations/hypotheses. Current ordinary Solver responses
remain complete and directly readable; no model-side character-slicing task is
introduced. A prior diagnosis may already be repaired or contradicted by a later
temporary parent and must be checked against current observations.

The existing Optimize worked reference is supplied separately for each Mutation
example using the existing 4096-character Unicode prefix with source, original
length and truncation labels. This is the dataset solution, not Solver writing.
No raw SearchValidation/Probe question, answer or trajectory enters Reflection;
no non-Mutation diagnostic is exposed. The reference, current writing and root
diagnosis allow grounded interpretation without changing Gradient's primary role.

Up to three preceding local attempts supply closed statuses/failed-check names
and measured Mutation, validation-root and actual-parent counts. Rejected text,
global scheduling history and a candidate archive are not exposed. An abstention
changes subsequent attempt feedback but does not consume extra draws, create a
success, stop early or force an unsupported edit. Typed output-contract feedback
still refers only to the last rejected draw and retains all strict validation.

The input supplies exact remaining replacement-block capacity from the current
rendered prompt. Generation instructions favor a specific conditional repair,
appropriate concise rewrite and scoped use of prior experience. A small local
loss is not an outer admission decision; valid `NO_SAFE_EDIT` remains available.
All blocks and the unchanged strict output schema remain supported.

Existing dependency/copy/leakage guards remain, extended to the newly introduced
optimizer-only field names and raw per-example diagnostic text. Abstract correction
text remains a permitted transferable hypothesis, not a hidden Solver dependency.
No optimizer context is injected into Solver or aggregation requests.

## Version and evidence boundaries

Current identities are Binding V11, cache V11, composition V11, Memory V4,
Layer1 V4 and optimizer-input V12. The observed-response policy has a fresh
identity because its optimizer-input binding changed; Solver ordinary-response
projection and mathematical validity/correctness semantics did not change.
The V2.7 pre-cluster-prefix migration remains historical-only; a current contract
with a non-null continuation policy fails before execution. Historical schemas
remain parseable but do not permit current execution. No old Memory/checkpoint,
cache realization or authorization is imported into a new run.

Offline archive projection may compare new views with recorded old wire views
without new search, response sampling or changes to historical outcomes. It is
not a new experiment and does not identify optimizer efficacy. Mock consumers
can verify input-dependent wiring, not real LLM utilization. Future evaluation
must separately measure delivery, actionable content, utilization, candidate
quality/cost and final team effects. A fresh authorized factorial comparison of
Memory delivery and Layer1 inputs is the next mechanism study; no automatic
authorization, model-budget increase or gate relaxation follows from this work.
